## Current State

### Architecture (Simplified)

**3 interactions:**
1. **Fill All** (panel + popup) → fillPersonal + fillAIQuestions + fillLearnedRadios
2. **Per-field "F" button** (hover) → fills one field (textarea→LLM, others→profile)
3. **Clear All** (panel + popup) → clears everything

**No auto-fill.** No `isLoginScreen()`. No `watchFormChanges()`. No `fillExtras()`. No `fillProfileSections()`.

### What Works
- **Form detection**: `FormDetector.detect()` → personal, questions, selects, files
- **Fill routing**: `fillField()` → `fillSelect()` | `fillTextInput()` | `fillOracleCombobox()`
- **Oracle CX combobox**: 4-strategy chain (DOM → native setter → char-by-char → InputEvent)
- **Per-field buttons**: Small "F" button, shows on hover, smart routing
- **Learning**: `listenForCorrections()` + `saveLearnedCorrection()` + `fillLearnedRadios()`
- **SPA observer**: Re-injects per-field buttons on DOM changes, no auto-fill
- **Token tracking**: `TokenTracker` loaded as content script, records usage
